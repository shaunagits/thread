§ 03 · The process

Start with a conversation, not a commitment.

You do not need to know the answer before the first call.

Fit call

A free 30 minutes

Scope

What gets built, in what order, and what it costs. Agreed before any work starts.

Build

Handover

It is yours to keep

The software, the accounts and the documentation. I stay available if the business changes.
