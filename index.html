§ 01 — The problem

Nothing is broken. That's the problem.

Every tool you bought works. QuickBooks does accounting. Square takes payments. The scheduling app schedules. Each one is fine on its own, and none of them know the others exist.

So a person becomes the integration. Someone reads a number off one screen and types it into another. Someone checks four tabs to answer one question. Someone remembers to send the invoice. It never shows up as a line item, which is exactly why it never gets fixed.

That work is the thing we remove.

Typical intake The businesses we map are running six to eleven separate systems, with two to four people spending part of every day moving data between them by hand.

§ 05 — Work

Every project ships with its plate.

We document what we built and why, in plain English, so the knowledge lives with you and not only with us.

Specimen 001 — Landscaping & maintenance · Kailua, Oʻahu 18 staff · 7 systems · 9 weeks

Before. Jobs booked in one app, invoiced in another, paid through a third, and reconciled by hand every Friday afternoon. The owner's daughter spent roughly a day and a half each week retyping numbers between systems, and nobody could answer "what did we make last month" without a two-hour spreadsheet exercise.

What we built. A single operations panel pulling live from their scheduling, POS, and accounting systems, plus automation that generates and sends the invoice the moment a crew marks a job complete. Overdue accounts surface themselves instead of being discovered.

After. The Friday reconciliation is gone. Invoicing happens same-day instead of same-week, which pulled average time-to-payment down by nearly three weeks. The daughter went back to running sales.

§ 06 — About

Built in Hawaiʻi. Useful anywhere.

Thread is a small custom software practice based in Honolulu. We work with businesses across the islands and with clients on the mainland who'd rather hire a team that answers the phone than a firm that assigns them an account manager.

Being here shapes how we work. Island businesses tend to run lean, on tools chosen years ago for good reasons, with no appetite for ripping everything out and starting over. That constraint turned out to be the right way to build software for anyone.

The name is the whole idea: the connecting thread that runs through everything a business already uses, and turns a pile of separate tools into one thing you can actually operate.

Working with us Time zones are real. We keep overlapping hours with the mainland West Coast and East Coast, and we answer within one business day. Most clients never need a meeting that isn't a demo.
