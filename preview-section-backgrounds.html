A · true blueprint, faded

A real grid rather than stripes: a major line every 88px and a minor one every 22px, both from --color-rule at different strengths. The whole thing is masked so it fades out at the top and bottom, which is the single change that makes it read as a watermark instead of graph paper.

B · blueprint plus registration marks

As A, with a small cross at each major intersection. That is the detail that makes draughting paper look like draughting paper. Marks are --color-line at low strength, so they read at close range and disappear at a glance.

C · paper grain, no grid

No lines at all. A fine monochrome grain generated by an SVG filter and inlined as a data URI, so it costs no request and no JavaScript. Warmer and more tactile, and it does not compete with the hairline drawings.
